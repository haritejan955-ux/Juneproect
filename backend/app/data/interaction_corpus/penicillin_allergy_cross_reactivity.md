# Penicillin-Class Allergy Cross-Reactivity

**Severity: Contraindicated (in a patient with a documented penicillin allergy)**

All penicillin-class antibiotics (amoxicillin, ampicillin, penicillin V, penicillin G, and
related agents) share the same beta-lactam core responsible for most penicillin allergic
reactions. A patient with a documented penicillin allergy is considered allergic to the entire
class unless allergy testing has specifically ruled that out.

**Clinical management:** Do not prescribe any penicillin-class antibiotic to a patient with a
documented penicillin allergy. Consider a non-beta-lactam alternative, or refer for formal
allergy testing/desensitization if a penicillin is specifically required and no substitute
exists. Note that cephalosporins carry a low (~1-2%) but nonzero cross-reactivity risk and
should be used cautiously pending clinical judgment.

**Monograph ID:** INT-010
