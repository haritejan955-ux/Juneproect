# Statins Metabolized by CYP3A4 (e.g. Simvastatin) + Macrolide Antibiotics (e.g. Clarithromycin)

**Severity: Major**

Clarithromycin is a potent CYP3A4 inhibitor. Simvastatin (and other CYP3A4-dependent statins such
as lovastatin and, to a lesser extent, atorvastatin) is cleared largely through this pathway.
Co-administration can raise statin plasma concentrations several-fold, sharply increasing the
risk of myopathy and rhabdomyolysis.

**Clinical management:** Avoid simvastatin (or lovastatin) with clarithromycin; if a macrolide is
needed, either withhold the statin for the duration of therapy or switch the antibiotic to
azithromycin (minimal CYP3A4 interaction) or switch the statin to one with a non-CYP3A4 pathway
(e.g. rosuvastatin, pravastatin).

**Monograph ID:** INT-005
