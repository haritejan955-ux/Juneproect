# Warfarin + Aspirin

**Severity: Major**

Concurrent use of warfarin (an anticoagulant) and aspirin (an antiplatelet/NSAID) substantially
increases the risk of major bleeding, including gastrointestinal hemorrhage and intracranial
hemorrhage. Aspirin inhibits platelet aggregation independently of warfarin's anticoagulant
mechanism, so the two effects compound rather than overlap.

**Clinical management:** Avoid routine co-prescription unless a specific cardiovascular
indication (e.g. mechanical heart valve with concomitant coronary disease) requires combined
therapy under close specialist supervision. If co-administration is unavoidable, use the lowest
effective aspirin dose (75-81 mg/day), monitor INR closely, and counsel the patient on bleeding
warning signs.

**Monograph ID:** INT-001
