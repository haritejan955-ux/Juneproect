# SSRIs (e.g. Sertraline) + MAOIs (e.g. Phenelzine)

**Severity: Contraindicated**

Combining an SSRI with a monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI) can precipitate serotonin syndrome —
a potentially life-threatening state of neuromuscular excitability, autonomic instability, and
altered mental status caused by excess synaptic serotonin. Both drug classes raise serotonergic
tone through different mechanisms, and the effect is additive to the point of contraindication.

**Clinical management:** Do not co-prescribe. A washout period is required when switching between
classes: at least 14 days after stopping an MAOI before starting an SSRI, and at least 5 half-lives
of the SSRI (longer for fluoxetine) before starting an MAOI. If both appear on a current medication
list, this is a contraindicated combination requiring immediate pharmacist/prescriber review.

**Monograph ID:** INT-003
