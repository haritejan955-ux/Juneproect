# Lithium + Loop Diuretics (e.g. Furosemide)

**Severity: Moderate**

Loop diuretics promote sodium loss; the kidney compensates by increasing proximal tubular
reabsorption of sodium, which drags lithium along with it. This can raise serum lithium levels
into the toxic range even without a dose change to the lithium itself.

**Clinical management:** Monitor serum lithium levels more frequently after starting or adjusting
a loop diuretic, watch for signs of lithium toxicity (tremor, ataxia, confusion, GI upset), and
ensure adequate hydration.

**Monograph ID:** INT-007
