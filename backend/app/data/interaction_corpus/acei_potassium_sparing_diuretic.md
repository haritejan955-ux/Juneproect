# ACE Inhibitors (e.g. Lisinopril) + Potassium-Sparing Diuretics (e.g. Spironolactone)

**Severity: Moderate**

ACE inhibitors reduce aldosterone-mediated potassium excretion, and potassium-sparing diuretics
directly retain potassium. Used together, they can produce clinically significant hyperkalemia,
especially in patients with reduced renal function.

**Clinical management:** If co-prescription is clinically indicated (e.g. heart failure regimens
such as ACE inhibitor + spironolactone), monitor serum potassium and renal function within one
week of starting or up-titrating either drug, then periodically thereafter. Avoid in patients
with baseline hyperkalemia or significant renal impairment without close monitoring.

**Monograph ID:** INT-004
