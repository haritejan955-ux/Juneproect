# NSAIDs (e.g. Ibuprofen) + ACE Inhibitors (e.g. Lisinopril)

**Severity: Moderate**

NSAIDs inhibit renal prostaglandin synthesis, which blunts the vasodilation that helps maintain
glomerular filtration when renal perfusion is reduced by an ACE inhibitor. Together they can
reduce the antihypertensive effect of the ACE inhibitor and, in volume-depleted or
renally-impaired patients, precipitate acute kidney injury (the "triple whammy" is worse still
when a diuretic is added).

**Clinical management:** Use the lowest effective NSAID dose for the shortest duration, monitor
blood pressure and renal function, and avoid the combination entirely in patients with chronic
kidney disease or who are also on a diuretic.

**Monograph ID:** INT-008
