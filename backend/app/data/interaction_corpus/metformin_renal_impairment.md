# Metformin in Renal Impairment

**Severity: Major (dose/contraindication concern, not a drug-drug interaction)**

Metformin is renally cleared, and accumulation in the setting of reduced kidney function raises
the risk of metformin-associated lactic acidosis, a rare but potentially fatal complication.

**Clinical management:** Reduce dose to a maximum of 1000 mg/day with moderate renal impairment,
and avoid metformin entirely with severe renal impairment. Reassess renal function periodically
in all patients on metformin, and hold the drug around procedures involving IV contrast.

**Monograph ID:** INT-009
