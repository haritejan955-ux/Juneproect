# Warfarin + NSAIDs (e.g. Ibuprofen)

**Severity: Major**

NSAIDs such as ibuprofen impair platelet function and irritate the gastric mucosa, and many are
also weak displacers of warfarin from plasma protein binding, transiently raising free warfarin
concentration. Combined with warfarin's anticoagulant effect, this markedly raises the risk of
GI bleeding.

**Clinical management:** Avoid chronic NSAID use in patients on warfarin. Prefer acetaminophen
for analgesia. If short-term NSAID use is unavoidable, monitor INR and consider gastroprotection
(e.g. a PPI).

**Monograph ID:** INT-002
