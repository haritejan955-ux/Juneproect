# Digoxin + Loop Diuretics (e.g. Furosemide)

**Severity: Moderate**

Loop diuretics can cause hypokalemia and hypomagnesemia. Digoxin's cardiotoxicity is markedly
potentiated by low potassium and magnesium, so this combination raises the risk of digoxin
toxicity (arrhythmia, nausea, visual disturbances) even at digoxin levels that would otherwise be
considered therapeutic.

**Clinical management:** Monitor serum potassium and magnesium regularly; supplement as needed to
keep potassium within the normal range. Consider periodic digoxin level monitoring, particularly
after diuretic dose changes or in patients with reduced renal clearance.

**Monograph ID:** INT-006
